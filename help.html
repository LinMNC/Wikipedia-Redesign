Help and search tips

Searching

Start typing in the search box and matching titles appear as suggestions. On the results page, choose “Titles only” to narrow results. If nothing matches, the page suggests a corrected title and lists articles to try.

Reading

On an article, the “On this page” list stays beside the text as you scroll (above the text on narrow screens). It jumps to a section and marks the one you are reading. Boxes marked with a triangle, such as key terms and sources, open with Enter or Space. Links that leave this prototype end with an arrow and are announced as external.

Keyboard use

Press Tab to move forward and Shift+Tab to go back. The first Tab stop is “Skip to main content”. In the search box, use the arrow keys to pick a suggestion and Enter to search.

Accessibility

Text scales with your browser settings and reflows at 400% zoom. Colors follow your system light or dark setting. To report a problem, ask at the Help desk.
