Tung Tung Tung Sahur

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is an AI-generated character, an anthropomorphic kentongan (an Indonesian slit drum), made with DALL-E. It first became popular as part of Italian brainrot.

First appearance
28 Feb 2025
Creator
Fernanda Bagas Indrastata (Noxa)
Based on
The kentongan used to wake people for sahur, the pre-dawn Ramadan meal

Origin

The day before Ramadan began in Indonesia, Noxa prompted DALL-E to draw a sahur patrol drum with a face and legs. The “tung” in the name imitates the drum’s sound.

Games and legal dispute

The character was removed from Steal a Brainrot in Sep 2025 after a copyright dispute and restored in Nov 2025. In 2026 Mementum Lab claimed ownership; AI-generated images are generally ineligible for copyright, so the question is being tested in court.
