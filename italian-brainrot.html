Italian brainrot

Italian brainrot is a run of internet memes from early 2025: AI-generated, surreal creatures with pseudo-Italian names, spread on TikTok. Each clip pairs a strange hybrid image with synthetic Italian narration that is often nonsense.

Key terms

Brain rot: slang for low-value online content and what it does to attention; Oxford Word of the Year 2024. AI slop: cheap AI-generated media filling feeds.

Origins

In Oct 2023 memes showed Dwayne Johnson rhyming about absurd topics, including the nonsense phrase “Tralalero tralala”. The character Tralalero Tralala is widely seen as the first example. A post on 13 Jan 2025 showing an AI shark in shoes reached 7 million views.

Characters

  • Tralalero Tralala

    The first viral character, said to run and jump at superhuman speed.

  • Tung Tung Tung Sahur

    A wooden log with a bat, of Indonesian origin.

  • Ballerina Cappuccina

    A pirouetting ballerina with a cappuccino mug for a head.

  • Lirili Larila

    A cactus-bodied elephant in sandals.

Reach

The characters drove a Roblox game, Steal a Brainrot, plus trading cards, a Panini sticker album and meme coins. Fortnite added Tung Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina skins in Apr 2026; players rated Ballerina Cappuccina the lowest of any skin.

Criticism

Some Muslim TikTok users objected because the original Tralalero Tralala audio contains blasphemy, though others say Italian speakers use such words casually. Bombardiro Crocodilo drew criticism for narration about bombing Gaza, raising concerns about desensitization.

Legal dispute

Mementum Lab, for the original prompters, and Do Big Studios, maker of Steal a Brainrot, are in court over who owns Tung Tung Tung Sahur, a test of whether AI-generated art can be copyrighted.
